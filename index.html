Biography

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Artificial Intelligence at Shandong University. I received my PhD from the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My research focuses on efficient and trustworthy next-generation AI systems, with an emphasis on computer architecture and systems software. My work spans fully homomorphic encryption, GPU systems, and specialized accelerators. I value dialogue with industry as a way to identify research questions grounded in real applications and systems.

Research

My research focuses on efficient and trustworthy next-generation AI systems. I study inference execution, privacy-preserving computation, and computing architectures, bringing together algorithms, systems software, and hardware design.

Efficient AI Systems

I study computation, memory access, and scheduling in AI inference. My interests include GPU operator execution, data reuse, and resource management to reduce inference latency and resource costs.

Trustworthy AI Computing

I study privacy-preserving computation and secure execution on sensitive data, with a focus on fully homomorphic encryption and its system support. The goal is to improve efficiency under explicit security constraints.

Computer Architectures for AI

I study GPUs, specialized accelerators, and heterogeneous architectures for AI and privacy-preserving computation. This work connects compute units, memory hierarchies, and dataflows to improve hardware efficiency and support different workloads.
