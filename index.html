Biography

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Artificial Intelligence at Shandong University. I received my PhD from the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My research sits at the intersection of computer architecture, privacy-preserving computing, and efficient AI systems. I study how architectural and system design can turn demanding security techniques into practical computing platforms.

Prospective students

I welcome PhD, master’s, and undergraduate students who are interested in computer architecture, privacy-preserving computing, fully homomorphic encryption, or efficient AI systems. I value intellectual curiosity, solid fundamentals, and a willingness to turn ideas into working systems.

If these topics resonate with you, you are welcome to get in touch by email to discuss possible research projects. damionfan@163.com

Research

I focus on efficient and secure computing systems, with particular interest in making privacy-preserving computation and AI workloads practical at scale.

01

Privacy-preserving computing

Architectures and GPU systems for fully homomorphic encryption and other privacy-sensitive workloads, with an emphasis on end-to-end efficiency.

02

Efficient AI systems

System support for learning and inference on sensitive data, with attention to computation, memory behavior, and deployment constraints.

03

Architecture and system co-design

Workload-driven design that connects algorithms, data movement, and runtime execution to improve performance without weakening security goals.
